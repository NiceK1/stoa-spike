Three kinds of drawing, each answered its own way

Blueline picks the method from the drawing. Where the drawing holds exact geometry it measures; where it is only an image, detectors we trained find the symbols. Every answer shows what was counted.

Building modelsIFC, and the plan sheets rendered from them Uses the quantities stored in the model, or measures its geometry storey by storey. 99.3%of 3,062 questions correct Doors on a basement storey
Scanned floor plansPNG and JPG images Two object detectors we trained on CubiCasa5k find doors, windows, fixtures and room types, and box each one. 85.9%of 1,781 questions correct, on plans the detectors never saw Doors on a scanned plan

With a vision model voting alongside the detectors, scanned plans reach 88.1%. This online demo runs the detectors on their own, with the answers to 224 drawings computed in advance.
